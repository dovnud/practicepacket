# 20. How a new grade 1–3 math site can win

Date: 14 November 2026. Research only. Nothing was built, bought, or registered. Detail is in files 21–26. The NSC score from file 00 stays **5**. This file says what would move it.

## The situation

On the Google pass that morning, the number-one result for the narrow queries was an incumbent, and a printable was in the top five every time. An AI Overview sat above those results and explained the skill. It did not hand over a worksheet. The newer clone domains were not on that page.

The pages themselves, fetched the same day, are not broken. K5’s quarter-hour page is already one skill, with several PDFs, at about 100 KB of HTML. Math-Drills’ subtraction and addition URLs are catalogs (about 4,900 and 5,800 words). K5’s FAQ says the answer key is on page 2 of the PDF, and that a preview, a separated key, and Google Classroom sharing are the $23.95 membership. Math-Drills’ 2-digit subtraction leaf says it was last revised on 2023-02-10. PageSpeed Insights returned HTTP 429, so nobody’s Core Web Vitals were measured. “We will be faster” is not yet a finding.

Pinterest’s share of competitor traffic was not published. Super Teacher’s Similarweb page lists Pinterest second among social sources, behind YouTube, with no percent. Search and direct are the channels with numbers.

## How we win

Win the document, not the head term.

Ship one URL per skill. The default PDF and the key are prebuilt, checked, and stored in R2. The HTML shows one preview, one worked example, and three labeled levels. A button can draw a new set without a new URL. No login. The grade-2 quarter-hour page is the control, because K5 already does that page well. The catalog queries (2-digit regrouping, facts, the grade menus) are where a single-skill page is a clearer result than what ranks today.

Do not spin fifty near-duplicate URLs. Google’s scaled-content policy, updated 2026-08-28, is about many pages made to rank rather than to help. Fifty skill pages with a real worked example are on the safe side of that line. A generator that publishes every redraw is not.

Do not count on Pinterest, Facebook groups, district pages, or a TPT dump. Those are either unmeasured or pointed at someone else’s domain. The test is still Search Console, 90 days after indexation, 500 impressions or stop.

Ads wait. A short skill page cannot hold a recipe-site ad stack, and Mediavine’s own guidance says the first screen is where LCP is decided. One reserved slot under the buttons, later, if a network will take the site.

## What would change the 5

Up, toward 6: any of the first 16 URLs in file 26 (the catalog openings) reaches page one or draws steady impressions, while the quarter-hour control also gets impressions. That would mean both the structural gap and the friction gap are real.

Down, toward 4: the 50 URLs are indexed and the 90-day impression total stays under 500, or a manual action cites scaled content. That would mean the incumbents’ age and links matter more than a cleaner page.

Stays a 5: impressions on the brand-new pages only, and nothing on the queries K5 already answers with a skill page. That is a niche of leftovers, not a site.
