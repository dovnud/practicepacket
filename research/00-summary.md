# Decision summary

Research date: 14 November 2026. No site was built and no domain was bought. Figures are verified on a cited page or labeled as estimates. Sources are in the numbered files.

## NSC verdict: 5 / 10

The niche is real. It is a poor bet as a copy of K5 Learning, and only a conditional bet as a narrow, print-first math site.

What is verified: Semrush’s public pages, last updated 11 November 2026, show October 2026 visits of 4.71M for K5 Learning, 2.86M for Super Teacher Worksheets, and 1.11M for Math-Drills. Similarweb’s public pages are lower and do not show that October spike. superstarworksheets.com serves a Mediavine ads.txt (marked v20261016) and Semrush shows 1.34M October visits, 1.13M organic.

What makes this a 5, not a 7: the head terms are already owned. A US Google check that day, personalization off, put K5, Education.com, Math-Drills, Super Teacher, and Common Core Sheets in the top five for the narrow queries, with a printable in every top five. Newer clone domains appeared in a different search tool, not in those Google top fives. An AI Overview was on all 10 queries and only explained the skill. It did not embed a download. Display RPM for worksheet pages was not found. Journey by Mediavine lists “excessive use of AI” as an automatic disqualifier. Raptive, below 100,000 pageviews, asks for long-form on most pages.

The score goes up if a 50-URL test draws impressions within 90 days of indexation. It goes down if those URLs stay invisible, if Google treats them as scaled content, or if Overviews start including the worksheet. On this check they did not. Google Trends, estimated from the chart, shows an August–September peak, a June–July dip, and holiday terms that spike for one month.

Semrush, October 2026: K5 4.71M visits, 8.65 pages/visit, 7:38, organic line “3M”; Super Teacher 2.86M and 7.83 pages/visit; Math-Drills 1.11M and 5.19 pages/visit. It does not hold for Twinkl.com (US 58.5%) or Worksheetfun (Similarweb: US 64%).

## Money

The working assumption of $8–20 per 1,000 visits is plausible for AdSense and too low for a premium network, if sessions stay deep.

Thresholds fetched 14 November 2026: Journey, at least 1,000 premium sessions in 30 days (Mediavine’s help page also says full Mediavine is $5,000+ a year in ad revenue). Raptive, updated 26 September 2026: 25,000 pageviews a month, 50% from the US, UK, Canada, Australia, or New Zealand under 100,000 pageviews (40% above that), and long-form on most pages while under 100,000.

No Mediavine or Raptive education RPM was published. Productive Blogging’s 2026 survey (129 bloggers, 2 January–29 April 2026; mostly food, lifestyle, and travel) defines RPM per 1,000 pageviews. The article text says ads as the main stream average $33.16, and Journey averages $28.60 versus $11.15 in its 2025 survey. At 5 pages per visit that is about $165 per 1,000 visits. A one-page PDF download is a different business. K5 and Math-Drills run a single AdSense line. Super Teacher has no ads.txt. Common Core Sheets is on Playwire.

Amazon.com Associates, fetched 14 November 2026: physical books 4.50%, toys and baby products 3.00%, everything not listed 4.00%. Office products are not a named row. That will not carry the site. Teachers Pay Teachers is a seller platform (Basic: 55% payout after a $29 fee; Exclusive: 77.5% after $59.95/year, help article updated 1 October 2026). A public commission for referring TPT buyers was not found.

## What to build, if the test is run

One skill, one URL, several print variants: `/grade-2/math/subtraction/2-digit-regrouping/`. The HTML page is what should rank. PDFs and keys are generated from the same problem list and stored in R2. Cloudflare’s docs (updated 5 September 2026) cap a free Pages project at 20,000 files and a paid one at 100,000.

Phase 1 is about 200 grade 1–3 math PDFs on about 50 topic URLs (regrouping, place value, quarter-hour time, grade 3 facts and fractions). Not 200 near-duplicate URLs. Judge it in Search Console 90 days after most URLs are indexed: impressions and top-20 rankings, not revenue. Kill line: under 500 impressions, or a scaled-content warning.

Phase 2, only if the test ranks: grades K–4 math (about 150–250 topic URLs) and one seasonal pack. Phase 3: K–5 math, then a small reading probe. Do not aim at Super Teacher’s 57,480 sitemap URLs.

Standards: quote Common Core text only with the NGA/CCSSO 2010 notice, and do not copy their examples. Do not paste TEKS or Virginia SOL text. Tag the code, link to the state, write the skill in your own words. Do not put “Common Core” in the brand. Public contact is `contact@<domain>` only.
