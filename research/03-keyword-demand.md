## Narrow clusters worth a test, and why

Chosen because autocomplete shows the phrase, the skill is a Common Core grade 1–3 boundary, and a single page can be made so that every problem really is that skill. Volumes: not found. “Weak SERP” is not claimed. File 02 lists new sites already on these phrases.

Grade 1

- Addition and subtraction within 20, split into within 10 and within 20. Autocomplete puts word problems and greater/less on the grade 1 stem.
- Greater than / less than. Suggested, and easy to do badly (pictures versus symbols). A clean symbols page is a narrow target.
- Telling time to the hour and half hour. The half-hour suggestion sits next to quarter hour.

Grade 2

- 2-digit addition with regrouping, and a separate page without regrouping. Autocomplete has both the skill and “with answers”.
- 2-digit subtraction with regrouping, plus a page that borrows across a zero. Autocomplete: “regrouping subtraction with zeros”.
- Telling time to the quarter hour. The suggestion list is the brief’s example, confirmed.
- Place value, hundreds. Suggested at grade 2.

Grade 3

- Multiplication facts, then a “100 problems” variant only as a printable version of the same URL, not as a second URL. Autocomplete has both.
- Division facts, which ride with “multiplication and division” on the grade 3 stem.
- Fractions. The grade 3 stem is a suggestion of its own.
- Rounding to the nearest 10 and 100. Not in the suggestion sample. Included because it is the adjacent grade 3 skill, not because demand was measured. Mark that as curriculum logic, not demand.

Reading probe, not phase 1: CVC words, with “answers” and grade 1 / kindergarten in the suggestions. Science grade 4 is real autocomplete and should wait. Writing did not surface in the math-heavy suggestion set. Not found is not the same as zero demand.

## What not to chase in a test

- “math worksheets”, “kindergarten worksheets”, brand terms, calculators, “educational games”. Owned, or a different product.
- Coloring-page variants as separate URLs. Autocomplete wants them. They are a seasonal or reward format, and they duplicate the skill page.
- One URL per random seed of the same 20 problems. That is how the clone wave will be filtered, if Google filters it.
