# 11. Page-count plan

Date: 14 November 2026. Counts below are a production plan, marked ESTIMATED. They are not search volumes. The comparison numbers from competitor sitemaps are verified counts of URLs, not of printable files (file 01).

## What the incumbents actually publish

| Site | Sitemap URLs on 14 Nov 2026 | What those URLs appear to be |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Super Teacher | 57,480 | A large mixed library. Brand search is a big share of traffic. |
| Education.com | 107,777 total, of which 30,235 are in the two worksheet-detail sitemaps | Worksheets plus games, exercises, workbooks. |
| Math-Drills | 7,665 | Topic pages for a math generator. |
| K5 Learning | 5,689 | Smaller than the visit count suggests. |
| Common Core Sheets | 4,189 | A generator site with a modest URL count. |
| Math-Aids | 1,320 | Topic indexes, including algebra. |

The visit leaders are not the URL leaders in a simple way. K5 has millions of visits and under 6,000 sitemap URLs. Superstar has about 1.3M Semrush visits in October 2026 without a sitemap that could be fetched. Matching Super Teacher’s 57,000 URLs is not the mechanism.

## Phase 1 — the test

ESTIMATED: about 50 indexable skill URLs and about 200 PDFs.

- Grades 1, 2, and 3, math only.
- About 4 variants per skill (two number sets, a support set, an extra set), one canonical URL.
- Hubs: three grade pages, three math pages, and the operation hubs those skills need. About 15 hub URLs. Indexed only when they have real child links.
- No reading, no science, no holiday pack, no membership.

Detail of which skills: file 12.

## Phase 2 — only if phase 1 earns impressions

ESTIMATED: on the order of 200–250 skill URLs, and 800–1,200 PDFs.

- Fill kindergarten and grade 4 math. Kindergarten is in the autocomplete stems (file 03). Grade 4 place value and multi-digit work extend the same generator.
- Fill the grade 1–3 holes the test skipped (measurement, money, graphs, area).
- One seasonal pack, kindergarten through grade 3, four holidays at most (Halloween, Thanksgiving or winter, Valentine, end of year). About 40–60 additional skill URLs if each holiday has a handful of skills, or fewer if a holiday reuses an evergreen skill with a new PDF on the season path. Prefer fewer URLs.
- A reading probe of about 30–40 CVC and short-vowel URLs, not a reading site. Autocomplete supports CVC. The rest of reading is a different production system (passages, not arithmetic).

Stop phase 2 if the new sections do not get impressions while the phase 1 URLs do. That means the generator is fine and the new topics are not, or the site’s authority is capped. Adding 1,000 more URLs will not fix a cap.

## Phase 3 — a library, still not a Super Teacher clone

ESTIMATED: 600–1,000 skill URLs across K–5 math, plus a small grade 6–8 set (fractions, integers, expressions) on the order of 80–120 URLs. Writing and science stay out until math has a year of Search Console data, unless the reading probe in phase 2 clearly worked.

At 4 PDFs per skill, 1,000 skills is 4,000 PDFs. In R2, that is nothing (file 10). As Pages files, it is a large share of the free 20,000-file cap once images are counted. R2 either way.

Do not plan a 20,000-URL phase. Education.com’s 30,000 worksheet URLs and Super Teacher’s 57,000 URLs come with authority scores of 65 and 51 and five-figure referring-domain counts. A new domain publishing that many pages in a year is the pattern Google’s scaled-content wording describes. The clone sites in file 02 are already trying it.

## Staffing shape, not a calendar

Phase 1 is a data and template job: one PDF renderer, one checker, one page template, fifty skill records. Phase 2 is more records in the same system, plus art for a season. Phase 3 is where reading passages stop being a template and start being editorial work. That is the expensive part, and it is why reading is not phase 1 even though CVC autocomplete is real.
