# 1. Competitors

Fetched 14 November 2026 unless a page date is given. Traffic figures are third-party estimates, not analytics. Semrush and Similarweb disagree, sometimes by a lot. Semrush “visits” jumped in October 2026 on almost every education domain checked, while the organic line on the same page was flat or down. Treat October visit totals as an upper estimate, not a fact about the school calendar.

Sitemap counts are URL `<loc>` counts downloaded that day. They include non-worksheet URLs. They are not a count of printable files.

## How to read the ad stack

`ads.txt` shows who is allowed to sell the site. It does not show revenue.

| Site | ads.txt on 14 Nov 2026 | What that implies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| k5learning.com | One line: `google.com, pub-0937566977556040, DIRECT` | AdSense-class. No Mediavine, Raptive, or Playwire. |
| math-drills.com | One line: `google.com, pub-2856036633404156, DIRECT` | Same pattern. |
| superteacherworksheets.com | HTTP 404 | No public ads.txt. Membership is the visible model. |
| teacherspayteachers.com | HTTP 404 | Marketplace, not a display site. |
| education.com | 37 lines. Google plus Amazon, PubMatic, Index Exchange, OpenX, Magnite/Rubicon, Sovrn, TripleLift. No Mediavine or Raptive manager domain. | A small header-bidding stack plus a paid membership. |

| math-aids.com, easyteacherworksheets.com, k12reader.com | Same header: owner domain `ampeducational.com`, manager `admetricspro.com` | One operator, three brands. A managed stack, not AdSense-only. |

## Large incumbents

### K5 Learning — k5learning.com

- Semrush overview, last updated 11 November 2026, October 2026: 4.71M visits (+97.75% vs September), 8.65 pages/visit, duration 7:38, bounce 32.07%. August 2.23M, September 2.38M, October 4.71M. Authority Score 62. Referring domains 17.41K. Backlinks 309.62K. Global rank 12,000.
- Country mix, same page: United States 68.25% (3.21M), Canada 5.44%, India 4.74%, Philippines 4.0%, Australia 2.71%. US+Canada+Australia = 76.40%. United Kingdom is not in the top five.
- Organic line on the same page: “3M”, down 1.47%. Paid search 0. The organic line is larger than September’s total visits (2.38M), so the two Semrush metrics are not one clean share.
- AI referrals, same page, share of total: ChatGPT 0.085%, Gemini 0.032%, magicschool.ai 0.017%, Perplexity 0.013%.
- US keyword table, October 2026: “k5 learning” volume 74,000, position 1; “cursive alphabet” 201,000, position 1; “math worksheets” 22,200, position 1, CPC $1.03.
- Similarweb page titled October 2026: bounce 29.84%, 8.64 pages/visit, duration 4:33, global rank 10,026. The compare module shows 3.5M. Country shares on that page: US 54.95%, Philippines 9.3%, Canada 8.14%, Australia 5.47%, India 4.11%. Organic search 64.74% of desktop visits. Traffic “decreased by 1.71%” versus the prior month. This contradicts Semrush’s +98% October.
- Sitemap: 5,689 URLs (5,000 + 689). Small relative to the visit count. Many worksheets may sit behind patterns that are not separate sitemap URLs, or the library is smaller than the brand suggests. A worksheet count was not found on the pages fetched.
- Price, membership page: $23.95 per year. Benefits listed include more worksheets, separate answer pages, no ads, and Google Classroom sharing. Cancel within 7 days and the card is not charged.
- Gap: math and reading are the core. The brand query is a large share of the keyword table. Cursive is a real side door (201,000 volume). Science is thinner in the public keyword list. Answer keys are a paid feature, which is a product gap for a free competitor if the keys are actually good.

## What the set does badly

Verified from the pages above, not from a survey of teachers:

- Answer keys are paywalled at K5. A free key is a real difference.
- Several “worksheet” brands get a large share of visits from a calculator, a game, or the brand name. The generic worksheet SERP is a smaller prize than the visit totals suggest.
- Reading sites in this set (K12Reader, Worksheetfun) show short visits (1:04 and 1:40 on Similarweb). Math drills hold people longer.
- Science and writing are claimed on homepages more often than they appear in the top-keyword tables fetched.
- No incumbent in the ads.txt sample, other than Superstar, is on Mediavine or Raptive. The default money is AdSense, a managed stack, Playwire, or a $24-a-year membership.
